# Round 61: Legislative Yuan terms for three candidates

This pass replaces undated résumé entries for Chiang Chi-chen, Su Chiao-hui, and Chen Ting-fei with the dates of the legislative terms in which the Legislative Yuan lists them. The term windows are:

- Chiang Chi-chen: 8th–10th, 2012-02-01 to 2024-01-31.
- Su Chiao-hui: 9th–10th, 2016-02-01 to 2024-01-31.
- Chen Ting-fei: 7th–10th, 2008-02-01 to 2024-01-31.

These dates refer only to Legislative Yuan service. They do not date the candidates' party positions, other offices, or professional work. Official pages list names by term and state each term's start/end dates. Chen's 7th-term listing and official term dates were checked; the 8th, 9th and 10th pages/listings also identify the three candidates. The official 10th-term page for Chiang separately states his 2020-02-01 start date.
