# Round 60: Ho Hsin-chun term dates

This pass fills year-level dates for Ho Hsin-chun's Taichung County council service, records the first post-merger city-council inauguration and her 2012 transition to the Legislative Yuan, and dates continuous LY terms 8–10.

The official LY biography gives her eighth-term start date (2012-02-01) and lists the previous county and city council roles, but those personal-history items have no individual end dates. CEC's 2010 Taichung City Council election bulletin describes her as a current 16th-term Taichung County councilor and a former 15th-term councilor. Official Taichung City Council archives list her in both terms. Year-level county dates therefore appear as 2002 through the 2010 county-city merger, with a note that exact personal handover dates are not documented in the consulted profile.

The Taichung City Government's contemporaneous notice states first-term metropolitan councilors were sworn in on 2010-12-25. The LY biography gives her eighth-term start date as 2012-02-01. As no consulted primary source states her individual city-council resignation date, the entry does not assert a verified 2012-01-31 end date.

Official LY pages provide the terms: 8th 2012-02-01 to 2016-01-31, 9th 2016-02-01 to 2020-01-31, and 10th 2020-02-01 to 2024-01-31. The displayed entry covers those three consecutive terms.
